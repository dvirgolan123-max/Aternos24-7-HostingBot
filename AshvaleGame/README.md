# ASHVALE — The Grey Fever

An original, hardcore open-world survival game for iPhone, written in Swift with a
custom Metal renderer and UIKit menus/HUD. You wake up in the Ashvale valley after
the Grey Fever outbreak with nothing but a T-shirt, jeans and sneakers. Everything
else — food, water, medicine, clothing, backpacks, weapons, magazines, ammunition —
must be found lying in the world, inside enterable buildings.

All meshes, textures, sounds and the map are generated by the game's own code at
startup. No third-party assets are used.

## Build and run on your iPhone

Requirements: a Mac with **Xcode 15 or newer**, an iPhone on **iOS 16 or newer**
(any iPhone with an A12 chip or later is recommended).

1. Open `AshvaleGame/Ashvale.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
2. Select the **Ashvale** target → **Signing & Capabilities** → choose your **Team**
   (Automatic signing is already enabled). If Xcode says the bundle identifier is
   taken, change `com.dvirgolan123.ashvale` to something unique.
3. Connect your iPhone, pick it as the run destination and press **Run** (⌘R).
   On first install you may need to trust the developer certificate in
   *Settings → General → VPN & Device Management*.

The project has one app target, is iPhone-only and landscape-only, and both Debug
and Release are compiled with optimisation so the game is playable from Xcode.
The first launch takes a few seconds while the world, meshes, textures and sounds
are generated.

## Game flow

`MAIN MENU → STORY INTRO → LOADING SCREEN → GAMEPLAY`

* **New Game** – cinematic story intro (skippable), then a fresh survivor.
* **Continue** – resumes the saved survivor of the selected server.
* **Servers** – three local server profiles, each with its own survivor and save:
  Regular; Hardcore (first person only, no crosshair, scarce loot, more infected);
  Survivor+ (more loot, fewer infected, slower hunger and thirst).
* **Settings** – graphics quality (Low/Medium/High), FPS counter, field of view,
  look/aim sensitivity, joystick size, invert Y, crosshair, day length, volume.
* **Pause** (II button) – Resume, Settings, Back to Main Menu (the game saves).

## Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left half of the screen | Floating joystick that appears where your thumb lands |
| Drag on the right half | Look around |
| **FIRE** | Shoot / swing / punch / throw (depends on what is in your hands) |
| **AIM** | Tap to toggle aiming (iron sights, red dot or scope) |
| **RELOAD** | Swap to your fullest magazine; with only loose rounds, refill the magazine (tap again to chamber) |
| **JUMP** | Jump, or vault over fences, walls and through windows |
| **CROUCH** / **RUN** | Toggle stance / sprint |
| **USE** | Open doors, pick up items, drink from wells and pumps |
| **GEAR** | Inventory |
| **1P/3P** | Switch camera (not on first-person-only servers) |
| Quick slots | Tap to take an item into your hands, long-press to clear |
| Ammo readout | Tap to switch fire mode |

### Inventory

Columns: **VICINITY** (items on the ground around you), **PLAYER** (HEAD, FACE,
TORSO, VEST, LEGS, FEET, BACKPACK, SHOULDER, HANDS) and the cargo grids of the
clothing you wear. Tap an item to inspect it and see its actions; double-tap for
the default action; long-press and drag to move it onto a grid cell, a clothing
slot, HANDS, a quick slot, VICINITY (drop) or onto another item (load rounds into
a magazine, insert a magazine, attach an optic/suppressor/light/grip, combine
stacks, refill bottles, …). Tap with a second finger while dragging to rotate.

## What is in the game

* **World** – 2 km × 2 km valley: the town of Halden, Brekka village, Northmill
  Works industrial area, Camp Vigil military base, the Route 9 checkpoint,
  Lake Silva, Greywood forest, Kettle Hills, farms, fields and roads with
  abandoned vehicles. 234 fully enterable buildings of 16 types (houses,
  two-storey houses, apartments, police station, hospital, shops, warehouses,
  barracks, garages, barns, gas station, tents, guard booths, containers,
  sheds, factories) with doors, stairs, floors, windows and furniture.
* **Physical loot** – every item exists in the world; pick it up, put it in your
  hands, drop it, throw it (with physics) and pick it up again. Loot tables per
  room type (kitchen, medical, police, military, …).
* **Survival** – health, blood, energy, water, stamina, body temperature, wetness,
  bleeding wounds, bandages/rags, wound infection, food poisoning, broken legs and
  splints, painkillers/morphine; death by infected, gunshots, bleeding,
  starvation, dehydration, cold or falls.
* **Weapons** – pistols, SMG, assault rifles, shotgun and bolt-action sniper,
  each with its own magazine/caliber, chambered round, fire modes, recoil, muzzle
  flash, sound, damage, durability and jams; loose ammo, manual magazine loading,
  unloading; visible attachments (red dot, 4× scope, suppressors, weapon light,
  vertical grip); melee weapons and fists.
* **Infected and bandits** – civilian, worker, police, soldier, runner and brute
  infected with roaming, hearing, vision, investigation, chasing, attacking,
  search and death states, door/stair navigation; armed bandits at military sites.
* **Day/night and weather** – sunrise to night with moon and stars, clear,
  cloudy, overcast, rain, storms with lightning and fog, ambient temperature.
* **Saving** – position, stats, clothing, hands, cargo, quick slots, every item in
  the world, opened doors, time of day, weather and previous bodies. Autosave
  every two minutes and whenever the app is paused or backgrounded.
* **Rendering** – Metal: shadow mapping with PCF, terrain splatting, procedural
  material textures, foliage sway, fog, procedural sky, water, particles, rain,
  first-person weapon view model, frustum and distance culling, LOD for terrain,
  trees and building interiors, background terrain chunk streaming, instancing.
* **Audio** – synthesized gunshots with echoes, footsteps, infected voices, doors,
  wind and rain ambience, thunder and heartbeat.

## Project layout

```
Ashvale/
  App/       AppDelegate, SceneDelegate, Info.plist
  Core/      math, noise, RNG, mesh building, procedural textures, render scene
  World/     terrain, roads, building kit + furniture, props, vegetation,
             collision, navigation, world generator, chunk meshing
  Items/     item database, inventory/containers, world items + loot, item models
  Entities/  character rig and animation, player movement, survival, AI
  Systems/   game loop, items, combat, environment, save system, cinematics, input
  Render/    Metal renderer and GPU resources
  Shaders/   Shaders.metal
  UI/        HUD, touch controls, menus, inventory, settings (UIKit)
  Audio/     procedural audio engine (AVAudioEngine)
Tools/
  headless/   Linux simulation harness: gameplay tests + software-rendered previews
  linuxcheck/ Linux typecheck of the UIKit/Metal layers against API stubs, MSL check
  xcodegen/   project generator and app icon painter
```

After adding or removing source files, regenerate the Xcode project with
`python3 Tools/xcodegen/generate_project.py` (or add the files in Xcode).

## Development checks (Linux, Docker)

* `Tools/headless/run.sh tests` – builds the platform-independent game code and runs
  the gameplay tests (doors, stairs, collisions, vaulting, loot, inventory,
  magazines, shooting, infected, bleeding, eating, throwing, save/load, death,
  respawn).
* `Tools/headless/run.sh soak` – randomized long-play test (default 20 simulated
  minutes, `SOAK_MINUTES=…`): random movement, looting, inventory actions, combining,
  throwing, weather/time jumps, save/load round trips, deaths and a combat phase.
* `Tools/headless/run.sh bench` – per-frame CPU cost of simulation, scene building
  and culling at each graphics quality.
* `Tools/headless/run.sh frames` – software-rendered gameplay frames (first-person
  rifle/pistol/fists, third person, interiors) into `Tools/headless/out/`.
* `Tools/headless/run.sh preview` – software-rendered previews of the map, towns,
  interiors and characters into `Tools/headless/out/`.
* `Tools/linuxcheck/check.sh` – typechecks every source file, including the UIKit,
  Metal, MetalKit and AVFoundation code, against signature stubs.
* `python3 Tools/linuxcheck/msl/check_metal.py Ashvale/Shaders/Shaders.metal` –
  approximate syntax check of the Metal shaders.

These checks run without Xcode. The final iOS build and the shader compile happen in Xcode.
